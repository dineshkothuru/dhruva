HUMAN GATE - you are shown: (1) the per-requirement design cards, (2) the design critique's unresolved findings (red panel, with each affected card flagged 'reviewer objects'). Decide: approve as-is, reject/comment individual cards, use 'Revise with ALL findings' to adopt the critique wholesale, or type your own instructions. Only after your approval are the HLD/TDD documents written.

Your decision is recorded in the design document and outranks the critique: a card you approve is not reworked by the designer in later rounds, and a finding you overrule is not raised again as though you had not ruled on it. Where the reviewer objects to a card you already approved, you see the objection and the designer's answer to it side by side - the card changes only if you say so.
